TWICE

TWICE (트와이스) is a 9-member South Korean girl group under JYP Entertainment. The group consists of Jihyo, Nayeon, Jeongyeon, Momo, Sana, Mina, Dahyun, Chaeyoung, and Tzuyu. In August 2026, Jeongyeon and Chaeyoung left JYP Entertainment but remained members of TWICE. The group was formed through the survival show SIXTEEN and debuted on October 20, 2015, with their first mini album, The Story Begins.
